# Asset sources

## Generated community image

Final project asset: `dist/assets/community.webp`.

Created with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool, then copied into the project and optimized to WebP. Original output was preserved under the default Codex generated-images location. The people shown are generated illustrative characters, not real users or testimonials.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide editorial photo for a Vietnamese community app for older adults, no text or logos. Primary request: three Vietnamese friends around 65 to 75 years old, two women and one man, laughing warmly together in a sunny community garden, one woman naturally showing the others a modern smartphone. Everyday light linen clothing in pale cream and sage, joyful and capable, authentic skin texture and silver hair. Composition: landscape 3:2, medium shot with all three faces visible, soft leaves and flowering plants in background. Natural soft morning light, warm cream and green color harmony. Photo should feel candid and dignified, avoid hospital setting, conical hats, medical equipment, watermarks or text. Save image as a project-bound photo asset.

## Campaign images

New fictional-character campaign artwork generated with built-in image_gen, using the narrative poster as character reference for the supporting photograph. Full original PNGs are in `campaign/`; lightweight web formats are in `dist/assets/`. Prompts and tool mode are recorded in `campaign/IMAGE_PROMPTS.md`. No real participant, service provider, rating or testimonial is depicted.
